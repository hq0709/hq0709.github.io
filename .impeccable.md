## Design Context

### Users
AI researchers and potential academic collaborators visit Hanqi Jiang's personal homepage to scan research, publications, and news, then follow links to papers and projects.

### Brand Personality
Academic, precise, approachable. Communicate research clearly with restrained emphasis.

### Aesthetic Direction
Retain the existing light academic homepage and typography. The user prefers aligned section edges, modest heading sizes, and unframed text over repeated cards. Publication lists should be compact and image-free; blog articles retain their individual designs.

### Design Principles
- Align headings and content to consistent page edges.
- Group bibliographic information tightly, with clear separation between papers and years.
- Preserve authorship, venue, and resource links; make secondary detail expandable.
- Use one responsive publication component for desktop and mobile.
- Keep visual changes scoped to the requested sections.
